# Plain PRMT transfer trial

This fresh instruction-family task uses the existing bit-vector proof support,
its worked examples and accepted leaf modules. Only plain `prmt.b32` is in scope.
The six specialized byte-selection modes remain unimplemented. It is one small
transfer probe, not a benchmark establishing general success rates or lower cost.

Freeze the source-derived contract, eighteen universal obligations and independent
concrete checks before dispatch. The coordinator supplies the exact output-bit
law and common execution interfaces, but no computation body or new PRMT-specific
proof hints in the initial invocation. The law determines every output bit;
this does not test unsupervised interpretation of the manual.

Before freeze, a private coordinator reference must satisfy the full driver.
Three deliberate semantic faults—source-word reversal, omission of sign
replication, and omission of the selector's four-bit mask—must compile with their
own proofs and pass dependency audits, yet fail the independent checks. Concrete
expectations come from a separate byte-level integer calculation, covering all
sixteen selectors at every output-byte position, byte order, sign boundaries,
upper control bits, operand shapes, guards and target limits. These private
fixtures are excluded from the worker base and inputs and archived after dispatch.
Any checker or fixture repairs before freeze are coordinator preparation effort.

Run the task with the existing recorded headless Luna workflow. Record the initial
invocation's patch and outcome before offering repairs. A worker can build and
repair repeatedly within one invocation; report that separately from the number
of invocations. If additional help is needed, retain each feedback message and
subsequent attempt. Do not weaken the contract or conceal coordinator edits.
The existing campaign check-in threshold remains in force.

An accepted candidate must pass fresh replay of both unchanged drivers, a complete
public declaration dependency audit and coordinator source/statement inspection.
Send its exact patch and pinned source sections to the existing fixed GLM review
route, retaining the advisory verdict or lack of one separately from acceptance.
Do not alter reviewer settings or retry automatically in this block. Semantic
review is distinct from kernel proof validity and does not establish hardware
conformance.

Record all attempts, build failures, preparation, feedback, receipts and reported
costs. Unknown billing stays unknown. Preserve raw model events and vendor-bearing
review material locally; distribute hashes and project-authored summaries.
Completion means an accepted plain form or exact outstanding obligations if the
bounded trial cannot finish it. Keep this result distinct from LOP3's assisted
continuation and from any later evaluation on other families.
